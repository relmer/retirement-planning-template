## Rank every sale by tax cost per dollar of risk removed

| Source | Tax cost to sell | Do it |
|---|---|---|
| Inside 401(k) / IRA | **None** | First, right away |
| Taxable lots with basis near value | Small | Next |
| Taxable lots with low basis | 15% LTCG (+3.8% NIIT above $250K MAGI) + any state tax | Spread across years, within the 15% bracket |
| Future vests | Ordinary income at vest regardless | Sell at vest |

Get the **lot-level detail** (each lot's date, shares, basis) from the
brokerage before scheduling the taxable sales.
